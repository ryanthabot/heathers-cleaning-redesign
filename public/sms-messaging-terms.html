SMS messaging terms

Last updated 24 November 2026

These terms apply when Heather's Cleaning Services sends you text messages.

On this page

  1. When we text you
  2. Marketing texts
  3. Stopping texts
  4. Cost
  5. Delivery
  6. Your information
  7. Changes to these terms

When we text you

We text customers who have given us a mobile number, about their own booking. That includes confirming a price or a time, reminding you of a visit, letting you know that we are on our way, and asking or answering a question about the job.

Marketing texts

We do not send marketing or promotional text messages unless you have agreed to receive them. You can say no at any time.

Stopping texts

Reply STOP to any of our texts, and we will stop texting that number. You can also tell us by phone or email.

Stopping texts does not cancel a booking. We will contact you another way, using the details you gave us.

Cost

We do not charge for text messages. Your mobile carrier may charge you for messages or data, depending on your plan.

Delivery

Text messages can be delayed or fail to arrive. Mobile carriers are not responsible for them.

Your information

We use your mobile number only as described in our privacy policy. We will never ask you for a password, a bank card number or a payment by text message.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
