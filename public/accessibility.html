Accessibility

Last updated 24 November 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website and to get a quote from us. This page says what we have done, and how to tell us if something gets in your way.

On this page

  1. What we aim for
  2. What we have done
  3. Another way to ask for a quote
  4. Tell us about a problem

What we aim for

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA as our standard. They are an internationally recognised set of guidelines for making websites usable by people with disabilities.

What we have done

  • Every page works on a phone and on a computer, from the smallest phone screens up to large monitors.
  • You can move through every page with the keyboard alone. The link, button or field you are on is always clearly marked.
  • You can enlarge the text up to double without losing content and without having to scroll sideways.
  • Colours are chosen so that text is easy to read. We check the contrast of every combination we use.
  • Photographs have a description for people who cannot see them.
  • Every field of the quote form has a label, and the form tells you what is missing before it sends.
  • The website respects the reduced-motion setting of your device, and it keeps all of its content visible when animations are switched off.
  • The cookie choices can be made with the keyboard.

Another way to ask for a quote

If the quote form is difficult for you, call (905) 904-1566 or email heatherbond2024@hotmail.com. Heather will take your request from there.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website is hard for you to use, please tell Heather by phone or email. It helps to know what device and browser you were using, and what you were trying to do. We will look into it and get back to you.

Questions? Call (905) 904-1566 or email heatherbond2024@hotmail.com.
