Service agreement

Last updated 24 November 2026

These terms apply when you book a cleaning from Heather's Cleaning Services. They are written in plain language, so that you know what to expect.

  6. Payment
  7. Changing or cancelling

What this agreement covers

It covers the cleaning services you book from us: a standard clean, a deep clean, and a move-in or move-out clean. Asking for a quote does not create a booking. Nothing is booked until you and Heather have agreed on the price, the date and the time.

Asking for a price

You can ask through the quote form on our website or by phone. The estimated price shown in the form is a guide. It is worked out from what you tell us and from our listed prices.

Heather confirms your final price with you. It is based on what you have told us about your home, so if something important changes, tell us. The price may change too.

How a booking is made

Heather confirms the final price with you and agrees a date and time. You can agree by phone, email or text message.

If you book a regular clean, the price and the schedule you agreed apply to each visit, until either of us changes them with notice.

What we clean

A standard clean follows the checklist on our website. A deep clean and a move-in or move-out clean go further. A deep clean is priced for your home, and a move-in or move-out clean has a flat rate that depends on the size of your home.

If you would like something extra, tell Heather. She will tell you whether it can be done, and what it costs, before any work starts.

Before we arrive

To help us do a good job, please:

  • be at home at the agreed time, or arrange another way for us to get in
  • make sure that the water and the electricity are on
  • tell us about pets, alarms, parking and anything else we should know
  • put away money, jewellery and other valuables, and anything fragile or confidential

We take great care in your home. We cannot take responsibility for money, valuables or confidential papers that are left out, or for items that are fragile.

Payment

Payment is due on the day of each cleaning, unless you agreed something else with Heather when you booked. Heather will tell you which payment methods she accepts.

Changing or cancelling

If you need a different date or time, tell Heather as early as you can. Our cancellation policy explains how to cancel or reschedule.

If something is not right

If we missed something, please tell Heather within 24 hours. We will come back and clean it again at no charge.
